Frequently Asked Questions

General Inquiries

What services does Machinoza provide?

We are a comprehensive service provider specializing in electrical wiring, CCTV installation and smart automation systems for both residential and commercial properties. We also offer specialized design and installation for custom projects like fountain and pond lighting.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve clients throughout Colombo and the greater Western Province area. If you are located outside this region, please contact us to discuss project feasibility.

Electrical & Automation

Do you handle full home rewiring?

Yes, we manage everything from minor electrical repairs to complete property rewiring, ensuring everything is up to code, safe, and built to handle your power requirements.

What kind of smart automation systems do you install?

We install modern automation solutions that allow you to control lighting, climate, security, and access from your smartphone or central control panel, streamlining both homes and commercial spaces.

Can you design lighting for outdoor water features?

Yes. We have specialized experience in designing, installing, and maintaining custom lighting systems for hotel ponds, decorative fountains, and outdoor water features, using safe, waterproof, and energy-efficient fixtures.
